How Our AI Platform Works

We don't just provide a chat interface. Stratum uses an advanced multi-agent architecture. You connect your data sources (like Notion, Google Drive, or raw PDFs), and our specialized agents take over. The Scoping Agent structures the problem. The Research Agent pulls exact, cited evidence from your data. The Synthesis Agent applies the Pyramid Principle to draft slide storylines, and the Red-Team Agent critiques the output for logical flaws before you export it to a client-ready PowerPoint deliverable.
